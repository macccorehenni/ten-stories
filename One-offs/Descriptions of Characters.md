# [[The Ten and their Powers and Badges#Givly (Giv)|Givly]]

## Personality

She is the youngest of the Ten, and is very goofy as a result. She tends to be more outlandish in her suggestions to the rest of the Ten, though she knows this and doesn't suggest stuff much. 

Her upbringing with the Parents (her evil foster parents) made her in to a spunky yet routine-driven independent young lady. While she has been forced to mask it, any break in routine is the same as a major disaster. But with Anna and Emmersun's help during her first year, she became much less rigid in her ways, and now enjoys a party or two, while she mostly still follows routines to a tee. 

## Her mount, and why she selected it 

Givly chose Emmersun because, "he looked so cool and snuggly".

Emmersun had gone through the same exact grueling childhood as his handler. He knew full well that she needed someone to give a shoulder for her to cry on. He put himself forward beacuse, as he said, "I emphasize with her. Her mannerisms, her singing— her choice of song— it indicates to me grief, longing for the past."

## Quirks

You'll see that she and Emmersun have a tell when she is channeling his power. Givly doesn't normally channel through him when she cannot see him, and looks straightish at him, always staying in eye contact with her target, but side eyeing Enmersun at the same time. This may be because of Giv's trust issues. While Emmersun and Giv could more than likely channel at a distance of around 500 feet, they don't if they cannot see each other. 

Another quirk of Giv's is her insistence on having Emmersun be close to her while sleeping. This may be because she once again cannot trust anyone not to betray her like the Parents did. 

## Appearance

For a nine year old, Givly wears somewhat mature clothing. 

Her shirt normally is some variant on a yellow/brown/gold oversized shirt, with her bottoms echoing that color. Her hair normally is the same too: three ringlets on top, with long, messy bangs. She wears no makeup, having not been introduced to that before joining Iris and Ammareth at the College.
