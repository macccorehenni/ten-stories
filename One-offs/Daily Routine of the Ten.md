# Morning routine of the Ten 
## Iris
Iris normally wakes up at five and eats some cereal (always something sweet or salty) then prepares Elvy and herself to fly down to the City and get a scone and some hot chocolate from the Iron Brew in College City. Sometimes, she'll save up the allowance that she gets from Anna and go to the more spendy cafe in Ilford for a much bigger cup of hot chocolate. In any case, she and Elvy go through the forest in the Valley above the college for some maneuver exercises and some exercise for the aging dream elephant, then return to the dorm and read for a bit in her egg chair before starting her day.
