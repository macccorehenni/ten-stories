# College of the Unheard Orientation

_Dear Student_,

I'm your headmaster, Anna, welcoming you to (or back to) the College of the Unheard. It's the only college in Ethyria, and a musical _and_ magic college one at that!

Full of suspense and tragedy, situated on the bustling Illford Approch, the College is the place to be. 

Here's a few things to keep in mind.

From Elder's Grove: Take the Frostsummer Connect to Barrymore, then take the Ilford Connect north to College City. 

From Ilford: Take the Ilford Connect south to College City.

### Factions, teams and absorption

Every year, from this year on, every student of College of Unknown will not only be part of a two or three person _faction_, but a nine to thirteen member *team* with two or three factions. This year specifically, a second year team will absorb you into their folds, making you a member of the team. The Ten have ultimate authority as the leadership of the school if any disputes occur. 

### Who is "the Ten", and why do they matter?

Speaking of the Ten, my team, they are a pretty unique team made up of three factions, plus one more later this year:

- The Misfits— Myself, Yae, Celesteia, and Amber
- The Loud Ones— Iris, Givly, and Ammereth
- And the Mindplayers— Tarim, Costa and Caverlly[^1]
We have the unique position of leadership for the College and will be making all desicions therein. **All major disputes should come through me or one of the Ten.** 

## For returning students

### Regarding the battle of Minden and its fallout 

You may recall a battle during the graduation concert resulting in the death of a student of the Mindplayers faction. 

For multi-years, or those who heard of the battle of Minden last year, the now faction leader, Tarim, assumed blame and asked to join our team for our second year.

Soon after, the headmistress, Andele, resigned under disgrace and transferred power to me. This was very much a controversial decision on former headmistress Andele's part, but strengthened the Ten as a team even before they decided to band together. Andele allowed Anna, Iris, and later Tarim, to bond together with us and lead together. While I was incapacitated due to being deathtouched (thank you to the campus healers for healing me from what was previously unable to be healed!), Celesteia led not only my faction of three, but two other squads! Kudos to her. As one of my first actions as headmistress, and leader of her faction and team, I award her the Bravery II badge.
