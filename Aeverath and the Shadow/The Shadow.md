
It was a clear day, reasoned Yae, as she, the King and her now fiance, Ferris, talked on the turrets of Feydor Castle. This land was now hers! She told her fiance about the adventures she had had with Givly and Ammareth and the Parents, as well as Minden. It was a bit cloudy, she thought, but it didn't look cloudy enough to really warrant any alarm.

A few hours later, the clouds had gathered into a storm head. It was threatening to rain, but not quite yet. Yae went into the castle, which had mercifully _not_ been touched during the final battle four years before, right before Yae was supposed to have her coronation as queen of Feydor Forest. That charge, combined with her duties as a member of the Ten at College of the Unheard on Mythra, made her life busy but rich over the summer. She herself had traveled back from College City. It was a two day trip via taxi and carrige, but well worth it to be with her family. With the rest of the Ten scattered to where _they_ belonged— Celesteia and Anna back in Celestial Eisnore, the Outcasts to their homes in the Capitol Valley of Mythra, Giv and Ammy to their _biological_ families' houses in Ainsley, and the others to their homes here in Feydor or in Danaé— she really didn't want to find herself anywhere else. After all, her father was dying, or at least the man who she thought for the longest time was her father. The king and late queen— who died during the siege of Thaylmor— had raised the half elf well. As much as she could be expected to, she still considered— and called!— the King her father.

As she entered the village— covered from the rain in an ingenious pattern of stone outcroppings and fabric covers enhanced with a mixture of Ethyrian, High Forest, and Feydorian feybloom to make them fashionable and waterproof even in the strongest of storms— she encountered three kids excited to see her. When she first returned to Feydor after the Vanquishing of the Parents and the discovery of Ammy and Giv's real families, she was a hero. Now, though, she was just their _queen_, just to be respected, and conversated like that. As Yae reached the end of the castle town, her father's loud, commanding footsteps, now accentuated with coughing, sounded. Her "lame duck" King was in bad shape. The healer in izah17— she could never get him to call the doctor as such— had said he had cancer, which Yae could see. But right this moment, her father seemed to be in a mind of panic, and needing her help.

"The Trail of the Demigoddess towards Feyrun was closed today. No one can cross the Strait of the Fog of War to Secomber."

"This storm?"

"Yes, people… elves… faeries… they all started… turning on each other. It's almost like… the Lich is back."

Yae wielded her telekinesis to pick a stray piece of High Forest feybloom.

"Hmm, what do we do?"

"We close the remainder of the Trail. Now. Before this storm reaches the castle."

"Agreeed. Iros!"

"Yes, Your Highness!"

Iros was Yae's attaché. He never left her side.

"Go find Ferris and tell him to find me in the war room. Meanwhile, the Trail is closed, per my decree. Thank you, go ahead."

Iros saluted and went about her requests. She started to the war room, but stopped at a space she hadn't been in quite some time— where she had first met her friend, Sora.

Here stood her old sword, the one she had  swore that she'd never touch— Verdant Leaf, enhanced with both Feydorian and High Forest feybloom braided in the hilt and down the blunt edge of the blade. A familiar warmth washed over her, almost as if the blade was saying, "Welcome back." She took it from its pedestal and it immediately molded to her calloused hands, worn from two years of perfecting telekinesis. Which reminded her— how does _this_ much feybloom affect her wielding? She focused on a boulder to her right, swung Verdant Leaf, and wielded at the boulder. She was surprised to see it budge a bit, but not much. She was happy. If she wore her ceremonial-slash-concert outfit, minus make up, plus her corset she wore before the final battle in the war of Feydor, she should be able to pull together enough power to throw something big if the occasion arose. Satisfied, she pulled the scabbard from where it was sat and slung it over her shoulder. That too, molded to her hardened shoulders from riding her minor image of her father. Once again satisfied, she went to the war room to meet with her fiancé.

---

"This seems no good. How many people have turned?" Ferris frowned.

"According to my sources, about twenty from the fishing village, another twenty along the Trail, and we've got our first casualty here in the Thalymorite castle town." Iros shuddered at the implications that that had.

"So, let's close the village to all." The King grimaced, as this meant that his castle would not get any tourism. But he realized that it would probably not come.

"Yes, I'm afraid we should." Iros frowned.

"I'm on it. Yaereene, do you think—"

As the king asked the question everyone had on their mund, the Queen and King apparent nodded.

"In the meantime, your Majesty, what do we do?"

Yae thought for a moment, then replied to Iros, "You and Father stay here. Ferris and I'll go to gather Anna, Celesteia and  the rest of the Ten. We'll be fine, I have Verdant Leaf."

Ferris looked surprised."You brought _Verdant Leaf_ out?"

Yae nodded."Yes, yes. I did. I will need protection. Ferris, meet me at the gate. Use your royal provenance to help the guard understand that we are leaving. Thank you. Be safe, you two, and that's an order."

Iros and the King nodded. "Will do, my lady." Iros saluted and waited for Yae to release him.

"Okay, we should all go. Iros, Father. Stay safe. You guys are good to go."

---

As Yae and Ferris left Thaylnor castle town, the deserted and destroyed village that all but surrounded the castle town seemed ominous. The storm flared— the storm that seemed to whisper rebellion.

"Like the good old days running from that Lich," mused Ferris as they made their way to the portals.

"But now we're stronger." His fiancé smiled at him, fingering the hilt of her sword, mimicking her motions she would use to wield if needed. "I sure hope this poison— whatever it is— hasn't reached Ainsley."

"Why not?" Ferris continued to walk, as the portal hall came into view.

"Givly and Ammereth."

Ferris smiled, ruefully, and said, "I may not have any magic powers, but I do know they haven't fallen to this poison, Aerie."

Yae flinched, still not used to her fiancé's pet names. But, deep down, she knew that he meant good for her.

She wielded a small rock out of habit. When riding her minor image of her father, to keep her and her somewhat frail elven body entertained, she'd wield. Not enough to channel, but just enough to wake herself. She often had to catch herself doing that and stop here in Feydor, but not now.

Yae broke her concentration on her wielding and stopped and turned to her fiancé and said, "One of the first things I learned at Unknown is that _every_ creature has magic. Some have mindwork, some have elemental powers, but all but the most heartless, somewhere down, have magic. Right here in the heart. And you're no exception." She smiled and continued.

---

Soon they arrived in Celestial Eisnore and found Celesteia and Anna in their house.

"Yaereene?" Celesteia, with her red and blue hair tied up in twintails against her head, came in for a hug. "Excuse me, though. _Your Majesty_."

Yae simply smiled, breaking away from Celeste's embrace.

"I'm now officially the advisor to the Mayor of Celestial Eisnore now, I guess." Anna said, coming in for a hug. "If not the mayor."

"I bring bearing dreadful news though." Yaererne frowned, unsure how to break the news to the girls she shared all of her life with for two years.

"Feydor is once again in trouble. A storm has brought forth unrest like that of the Lich. As the two members of the Ten here in Celestial Danae, I thought you'd like to know."

Celesteia's smile faltered.

"Oh really? That's unfortunate." Anna started fidgeting with her hands, a nervous stim that not only Yae but also all three of the Loud Ones noticed, Givly going as far as to confirm as much through wielding.

"It really is." Ferris frowned, a facial gesture that had become more common on the couple's faces over the past few hours.

"Okay then, what do you suggest we do, sister? We can't just go to protect Thaylnor, we got business here." Anna moved over and put her arm around Celesteia, in a gesture that seemed to indicate that they, too, were now a couple.

"If I may, Anna." Ferris stepped foward, pushing the supposed Eisnoreian couple apart, and forming an air of authority.

"Sure, Ferris, what's your idea?"

"I talked to Celestial Danae's meteorological service— the storm is brewing here too."

"That's shit, really," Anna said.

"That's tame compared to what Danae in the Danaean Realms is going through though." Anna said. She turned on her TV. On it was Celestial Eisnorean PCR/DPR, the local public media TV station, which was playing a story on repeat every five minutes. "izah17 under attack by mysterious storm, driving area residents insane" was the hero line.

"Danaeans are under a strict stay at home order in many areas due to this mysterious storm. The only people who are authorized to exit their homes are governmental agencies who are certified Pokemon threat safe, as many people who have braved the storm have seen a mysterious portal-like eye to this storm.

The eye is only growing, and it seems to have engulfed the area of Minarta and is heading towards izah17 Area PCR/DP—"

Before the newscaster could finish her sentence a large, muscular Ultra Beast— Buzzwole— swooped down and snatched her. The feed went black, and no picture returned.

"That's not good." Anna said.

"You know what that reminds me of? It's a Hollow." Seeing blank eyes, Celesteia explained that that eye was the source of the corruption. She had been playing a game where these patches of corruption— facsimiles of the real world— were the focus. Intense monsters were found within, and, more importantly to what they were seeing in Danae, people corrupted into these monsters.

"So the Shadow is basically a patch of corruption?" Anna remarked.

"Yeah, basically."

"Damn, then."

Ferris, who had been silent to this point, chimed in with, "How do we stop it?"

Yae and Anna both simultaneously shrugged. "This is just as new to the mages and scientists as it is to you and me."

"We should loop in the rest of the Ten, as well as Danae and their allies. After all— the eye of that storm is getting painfully close to End City portals."

A half an hour later, fifty or so people sat around Castle-upon-Akala's biggest conference room that served as the Danaean capital building's war room. The twenty members of the Ten, repersenting Ethyria and the College, the five Kahunas who led Danae, Maccore, and several emmisaries from each nation allied with Danae, all were present.

"I call this meeting to order, then." Maccore, being the de facto leader of the meeting, given his postion as leader of the Multi-world Conference, nodded at Anna. "Everyone ready?"

"Yes, I think we are. Shall you take roll call, hm?" Anna asked.

"Not today. Your whole team here?" Maccore was the master of multitasking, and was finishing the draft of his team proposal as he did a check with all leaders.

"Yes sir."

"Then I'd reckon that's fine, then. Kahunaite?" he prompted the leaders of Danae.

"All present, Core." Kiawae, the leader of Danae, smiled at his friend, and fellow Fire-type Pokemon trainer.

"Great. Allied leaders, then." Maccore had closed his laptop— normally he'd use his smartglasses for his computer but typing was too damn hard.

"All present." Lymin, the Hisuian leader, and de facto leader of the nations in the Realms other than Danae, chimed in.

"Great, then shall we start? Yes, yes, we shall," he finished after a moment to gauge response. "with our pressing topic of the night."

"No doubt you have all seen the news on the Shadow Hollow that has been creeping ever closer to End City."

"Yes, the weird storm with a corrupted center." Lymin frowned, as did several other politicians around the table.

"And it's been corrupting people?" Damian, the Unovan leader and easily the weakest and the most nervous (perhaps except the Ten's Roxy, who was a chinchilla girl after all) of the assembly, was extra nervous as he wringed his hands.

"Yeah, that's what I'm getting." Givly, the youngest here by several months. "And I can tell you all are nervous, thinking, 'What are we gonna tell our people?'"

"How did you know?" Damian looked flabberghasted.

"I'm a mind reader," Givly said, matter-of-factly.

"Ah, yes, I forgot to mention my team has several mind-determining powers, so… you might be called out on any bull that you may to try to give." Anna smiled.

"Damnit! There goes my plan." Tolof, a genial, portly folk and the leader of Kanto, joked.

"Givly _does_ bring up an important point though. What _is_ The Ten, Danae, Feydor and allies' message."

"I declare a vote, between _don't worry_ and _panic_." Ammereth, Givly's sister, chimed in.

"Panic, I say. But more measured." Tolof said, serious.

"I speak for the whole of the allies when I agree with Ammereth and Tolof." Damian said, a frown worrying his lips.

"So we're all in agreement?" Anna asked all present.

"I'd like to think so. Any objections to skipping the official vote for all and making our statements?" Maccore said, in his official role.

"Sure, I guess. Ten's okay with that." Anna sighed, relieved.

"Yes, we agree." Lymin replied.

"Sure, let's do it. Who should go first?"
