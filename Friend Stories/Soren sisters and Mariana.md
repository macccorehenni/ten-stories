# Soren sisters and Mariana— Speaking of their Tragic pasts

"Hey, Mariana? I heard you were a pirate." Givly ran up to her friend and tapped her.

"Not— really, but I did live on a ship, more or less."

"Huh, how's that?"

"Well— let's just say my past wasn't fun."

"Well, that's cool." At this point, both Giv and Mariana were sitting in Giv's nest in Callendor dorm, fully attentive to each other. 

"I wish my past were that— that cool." 

A voice sounded behind the two, small yet somewhat confident, still learning to trust. Ammareth sidled into her sister's nest and laid her head on Mariana's.

"Giv and I—" she nodded towards her sister— "weren't very well taken care of. My mother died in her sleep, and Father drove herself crazy, eventually… slinging himself off our room. You know, teiflings aren't known to like those who crumble under… pressure. So I wasn't well received in my community, despite not having parents at this point."

Givly sighed, and shared the fact that her parents— her dragonborn father and human mother— had died in a accident with a car. This was right after the Ethryian portals were established and before cars were banned in Ethyria. 

"I was only six, too young, I guess. That was three years ago."

"What did you both do to _get here_?"
