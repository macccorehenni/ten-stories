# Finale

*At the College of the Unheard, students perform to advance: each song is a trial where music channels magic, and factions prove command over their own style of power. The Misfits—first-years bound to their mounts, creatures whose lives are knotted to their handlers’—entered finals knowing the rule everyone whispers: if a handler dies, the mount fades; if a mount is lost, the bond scars, sometimes forever. Tonight’s concert decides more than promotion to second year; it sets faction standing for the next cycle and marks which bonds the College will formally protect. Backstage, they waited, minds reeling. They had survived year one, despite their challenges. But they were not through.*

*They called their gift ‘wielding’—shaping power through melody and will—but prophecy had always shadowed their trio. All term, the Mindplayers, led by Minden, tried to break them, hunting for the one voice whose power even she didn’t know she held. And ‘Nightmares,’ the song the Misfits chose, had history: it exposes fear, disrupts control—an affront to Mindplayer doctrine. If the Misfits succeed, they embarrass—or destroy—Minden on the one stage that matters. If they fail, they repeat the year and cede the hall—and its protections—to their rivals, their strength unchecked. Their intent with this concert was to draw a line in the sand, not advance to year two. This is their story.*

---

"Listen up, team!" Anna said, her bright blue eyes belying a sense of dread and panic, backstage in the music hall of the College of the Unheard. What would happen if *any* of her faction was killed? Would they die, and their mounts after them? If one of them died, bringing down their mount with them, it would not reflect well on the college. Then again, she tried to reason with herself, they *would survive*. As she, Celesteia, Yae and Amber looked on as the Loud Ones went on stage, Anna continued, shakily, "We are the Misfits. We *will* survive, even if the Mindplayers try and attack us. Which at this point is likely. I've been observing Minden—"

Anna stopped, out of breath, as the youngest faction, the one with the closest bonds to the Misfits, the ones who themselves had fought off the corruption of the Mindplayers, came back. Each of the Loud Ones looked upset as their mounts each tried to comfort them, Emmersun going over to Givly and softly purring.

"We'll be seeing you next year—as first years again." Givly, the youngest of the trio, seemed heartbroken. Givly accepted Emmersun's snout without questioning it, allowing him to comfort her.

"Why? What happened?"

"Somehow my power knocked over the lighting lamp. We're not moving through." Givly replied, a sour taste forming in her mouth as she snuggled with her mount, his fur bright purple and orange, his love and devotion to his handler shown in the way he looked at her as she cried, her long, blonde locks once again covering her whole face.

Iris confirmed this, frowning as her elephant mount came up to her.

This statement seemed off, though. Celesteia knew that Givly's power didn't have any physical manifestation—she's a mind reader, her power not able to knock down any lights, only foretell their admissions. Something was off, for sure, and she frowned as she and her mount looked at each other.

Palden said to Celesteia through their own bonds, “It’s Minden, isn’t it?”

Anna thought for a moment, her eyes widening. "Huh. Waaait… Huddle up for a moment, Misfits."

Yaereene, Amber and Celesteia moved in to surround Anna.

"They—the Mindplayers—were trying to disrupt *us*, weren't they?" Anna asked her factionmates, much to Celesteia’s relief.

"Probably—I know Tairm has his motion powers, but someone *else*—"

"Sorry to interrupt, Miss Anna, but you know I can hear you, right?"

"Givly, you know better than to read minds without permission." Iris, the Loud Ones' leader, gently reprimanded her teammate. Then, lowering her voice with a hint of reluctant agreement, she added, "Still… she's not wrong. We do need to be more careful."

Anna smiled, perhaps putting on a veneer for her adopted family, the ones she cared for throughout the year, protecting them from Minden, the Mindplayers and the danger they were in. "Girls, don't worry about that, okay? We're safe without your help."

“You’re bluffing,” retorted Iris. “Givly told me that, and I trust her read.”

As if trying to add to the fire, Celesteia nodded. "Yes, she is bluffing, and doing terrible at it."

Anna glared at her, and then shook her head. "Okay, yes, I think we are in danger from them. But—what are you three?"

Givly shrugged, and her shoulders—and mount—sagged. "We are going to be repeat years?"

"I have faith that that will *not* be the case, Giv." Yae replied.

"I dunno bout that, Yae." Iris wasn't frowning, *per se*, but neither was she smiling. "But barring a miracle, we won't be moving on. But I may have a plan. If I may, Miss Anna?"

Iris tugged Anna’s sleeve, prompting her to follow. Her teammates had questions, but they trusted her judgment. Despite being only ten, Iris had shown remarkable poise and leadership—far more grace than the traitor who had tried to kill her best friend. Her face was grave as she looked up at Anna.

"Yes, Iris? You had something you wanted to converse with me about?"

"Are you *sure* you're okay and safe, Anna?"

"I'm fine, little one." Anna replied, before noticing Givly nearby.

"*Okay*. Fine, we may be in danger. But don't you worry about me, now.”

Anna smiled, but the weight behind it was immense. Her lips curled upward, a gesture meant to reassure, to deflect the rising panic in her chest. She had to keep the bluff going for as long as she could—because if she faltered, even for a moment, the others might crumble. Her smile was armor, a fragile shield against the chaos she knew was coming. Behind her eyes, strategies churned and fears clawed for attention, but she held firm. For her faction. For the mounts. For the College itself.

"The mounts agree, you know, Anna." Ildren, her gryphon, chimed in with an audible, loud clicking noise, through her bonds with each other and the other Misfits.

"If anything happens… we're going in." asserted Celesteia's mount, a unicorn named Palten.

"Can you hear me out, Miss Anna? I'd love to help you. I'm—"

"The way you and your faction can help, Iris, is by *staying safe in the greenroom*, girl."

Iris nodded, her confidence fading. There was much more Iris wished she could do, but Anna wouldn't let her. So she'd have to save her on her own.

---

Soon enough, it was the Misfits' turn to sing.

"Let's have a change of plans. Remember the song I sang for my tryout?" Anna asked her factionmates, searching their faces for any trace of recognizance, panic still lining hers.

"'Nightmares?' I've been practicing that song for a while now." Yaereene responded, her interest piqued.

"Well, we'll be singing that tonight."

Everyone frowned. Why the sudden change?

"Why will we sing this tonight, Anna?" Yaereene frowned.

"Only because it'll be more or less fitting, given the nightmares this night might bring us." Anna said through the bonds of the Misfits.

She had realized that the Mindplayers were serious, they wanted *something*, something that they had been fighting for thro—

"You trying to give us a subliminal message, Anna?" Celesteia asked Anna through bonds after she had not responded.

"I'm saying, have your mounts ready, hm?"

"Misfits, you're up, you're holding up several other factions here!" The stage master chided the quartet from the other side of the greenroom, ready to lead the somehow often-forgotten faction—to their death?

---

It's true—no one paid the Misfits any attention. They were just flies on the wall, as far as the other college students were concerned. With the exception of Yaereene, who used a minor image of her father, the Feydorian chief god, Danche, as her mount, nothing was special about the Misfits. They were misfits—never supposed to be here.

They bonded on this fact, and survived their first year. But—they quickly learned during this first year, that Minden was *not* a fit for The College of the Unheard. They were manipulating, paining their factionmates at every step. The name of their faction was their suggestion, or rather their edict. They acted like a dictator every step, every day, every decision their squad needed to make. The squad were oppressed, even being threatened with ejection from the faction. This was not what the College stood for.

And now, Anna and the other members of the Misfits strided onto the stage. Hearts heavy, they started singing, their mounts providing—

Suddenly, about halfway through, a crash sounded, followed by the deafened scream of the stage handler, and the Mindplayers came through the door with malice in their eyes.

Minden, the corrupt leader of the faction, came in and sang the middle verse of the song, indicating his malice and his desire:

> What have we here, little ones?  
> Did you really think that you could defeat me?  
> I spy with my  
> big red eyes  
> a feast for me  
> in youthful splendor.

Anna choked up as they moved closer. So, this was it, thought Anna, but the Mindplayers suddenly stopped, taunting Anna from afar.

“It’s your line, Anna Ildren. What’ll it be?”

They tilted their head, waiting for Anna to continue, pure anger in their eyes.

Backstage, the Loud Ones and the Misfits' mounts stood in uneasy silence, their ears tuned to the chaos beyond the curtain. Despite the desperate cries of their handlers, they remained still, bound by rules and fear. Each heartbeat after the Mindplayers—save for Minden, thank the Gods—shoved past toward the stage throbbed with tension, like a drumbeat of impending doom. The Loud Ones were worried about their friends, and as much as they wanted to intervene they did not.

The mounts were talking amongst themselves, and Givly, though she couldn't hear what they were talking about, could tell they were tense and just as worried as she and her factionmates were about their handlers.

"We *do not* intervene during this concert! The handlers' professors explicitly forbade the entering of mounts!" Ilven, the most level-headed yet rule-following of the three faction mounts, glared at his fellow Misfits faction mounts.

"Yet, Ilven, our handlers—not to mention, my daughter—are in peril. Perhaps we shall intervene for their safety." Danche chipped in, his hooves grating against the stone floor.

"Sorry, Ilven, gonna agree with Danche. Normally I'd lean towards agreeing with you as the faction leader's mount, but why risk my beautiful two-tone haired girl's life? I'd rather give my own life—or wander, aimlessly, after being expelled by the College—than *die*. Choose what you want, Ilven, but, I *will* save Celesteia. No questions asked, are we going?" Palten sighed, seeing no movement in the gryphon's stance to indicate he agreed, but brightened when he saw him consider for a single second.

"Honestly, if I were you four?" Emmersun smiled, his fangs baring as he stood at his half-dragon. "Go. All three. If your handlers need to wield—and they will—you will need to help them."

"We will not go and help." Ildren stood and turned away from Emmersun, finalizing the conversation for all four of Misfits' mounts.

But a scream that sounded quite a bit like Anna's screamed out. All three mounts of the Misfits, and the three members of the Loud Ones and their mounts, ran out onto the stage to see Anna down on the ground, lifeless.

Minden stood over her, their face contorted into what one would only hope was a smile, at *thinking* they had won. But then Iris stood behind them.

---

"How dare you kill my… best friend!" Iris screamed, wielding her magic at her fullest potential, her dream elephant mount, Elvey, helping as much as he could.

Minden stepped up to Iris, and their team followed them.

"Oh, you little annoyance. Ten is too young to be at this college, and you know it." Minden launched themself towards Givly, but Emmersun blocked the attempt by moving in front of his handler. He growled with the full force of a grown tiger, despite only being half Minden's size.

"I'd be careful if I were you, Minden." Givly said, trying to probe their thoughts, but failing.

Minden moved towards Anna, passing Yaereene as they sneered at the half-elf, demigoddess.

"Iris? Giv?" Yaereene looked confused, but then remembered—Minden wanted her dead.

Enraged and having the upper hand, Ilven struck with his barbed tail against Minden. They fell, but only just long enough for Iris and Emmersun to move out of harm's way. Their target lost, Minden moved over to attack Yae, who immediately wielded and threw a stone at them, knocking them off balance long enough for Yaereene to wield again. Before she could fully heave the light (she was struggling, as it was a bit out of her power's range), she was swept off her feet by Tarim—who looked to have been forced by his faction leader to wield his magic.

Luckily for her, Ammareth slowed her fall and allowed Yaereene to finish her wielding and knock down Minden, but they got up.

No matter what they tried, the Misfits and Loud Ones could not stop Minden or their factionmates they controlled. But suddenly, Ilven cried out in joy as his handler woke up, only to channel through him to cast a lightning bolt that finally did knock down the miscreant.

Yae was then able to pick back up the stone and throw it at their bloodied body, dealing the final blow and killing them. Anna was alive, but didn't look well. The poison didn't work, or did it? Regardless, the show had to go on.

After the song finished, Anna got taken to the hospital, with her final words through the bonds of magic shared between being, *"You're in charge, Celesteia."
