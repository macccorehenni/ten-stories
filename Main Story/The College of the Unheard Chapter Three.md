# Chapter 3— The Shadow Arrives
It had been a few weeks since they had saved Ammareth from the house in Aisney. She had been badly bruised and found it painful to even walk. This reminded her of when she and Givly had ran away from the Parents. Still, Ammy and Giv both had premonitions that that would not be the last time they met the Parents. 

"Hey, Givly?" Ammareth's little sister hadn't left her side since they returned from the Parents' house, and she now laid across her sister's lap, snuggling, as the sun set. The teifling and half dragonborn hadn't been close like this since they ran from the Aisney house what seemed so long ago now.

"Hmm?" Givly smiled, happy to hear her sister's voice. She had been scared that she would never see her sister again. 

"I'm glad I found you. Was it a mistake? No, not really, I feel. Maybe it was, but, honestly, that is in the past. But now— *now*, you are in danger too. I may only be eleven but I know… I know that we, _we_— both of us— aren't out of danger yet."

"You know, sister, I'll _always_ be with you. You don't have to ever, ever worry about that."

Ammareth sighed. "I do love you, too, Givly. But I still feel you are putting your life at risk doing this, sister. I've put you in enough danger as is, sister."

Givly allowed herself a small, shaky sigh. "You aren't wrong, but… I still feel like I have to protect _you_, Ammy. Just as much as Avery expects to be by your side, forever."

As if prompted by the half dragonborn, Avery lifted his head to say as much. "Little ones, I would never dream for any danger to you, or any of your team… and I only forsee that to grow like it has already. Danger may come, but your sister, red-haired, do you _truly_ trust her?"

But did she? It would seem she did, but Ammareth realized that she more than understood what Avery wanted her to actually answer. He wasn't talking about her sister, per se, but _herself_. If push came to shove, if her innocent sister turned on her, whether by conscious decis1ion, or by force, would she be able to trust herself? She still doubted it, despite her unwavering love she saw from both Givly and Avery, as well as the rest of her friends that she had made over the last three years. But instead of telling Avery about her newfound insecurity, or even telling her sister, she began to sing a song, one that she often sang in those long nights, where her arms were tied up, listening to the half dragonborn before her cry out in pain, when she was thrown back into the dungeon— the basement she herself had called home for a week more than her sister, her long blonde locks draped across her chest now, eyes fluttering as she struggled against sleep in Callendor, safe from the torment and slings she and Ammareth had encountered. She began to sing the song that she herself had sung three years before, "I Better Be Quiet Now" by Elliot Smith. She choked when she got to the part where she sung, with her raspy, prepubesent voice, half breaking:

> Maybe I have a problem, but that's not what I wanted to say
> I'd prefer […] to say nothing…

Then she broke down, crying. Not the kind of happy tears that she had been more used to crying over the last few years, but sad, ugly tears. A few minutes later, Givly sat up, just to find her sister asleep, tears staining her golden brown cheeks, her sorrow drowned, for now, by sleep. She smiled, softly, half-asleep, then got up and returned to her own nook, where Emmersun waited to sleep with his handler. She slept soundly, perhaps for the first time today since returning from Aisney.

---
Givly woke to her sister playing with Avery as they both ate their breakfasts. Everyone seemed to be in good spirits, and their day was normal. As Giv looked out at the sky through her skylight, something felt… off. "Huh, something's definitly gonna go down today," she remarked to Emmersun as she prepared to start her day. Emmersun's fur looked brighter than usual, but Givly didn't notice at all. She jumped down from her nook, avoiding the stairs as she usually did for her first descent from her bed. 

"Morning, Givly! How are we today?" Her sister, as was normal, was the first to greet her, followed by Anna. Somehow, she and Celesteia had very… _shiny_ hair, something both young girls envied. Each had attempted to ask their friends how that was, but both Anna and Celesteia shrugged it off. But Givly sensed something was off about their hair today. Was it _more_ or _less_ shiny? In any case, Givly dismissed this as just her hyperactive brain. The exchange that she, Ammareth and Avery had two nights before still stung her mind. But, she reasoned, Ammareth was safe? Right? _Right_? 

Yet, Ammareth had said that both she and Givly were still not out of trouble's way— which was quite disturbing to the half dragonborn. Either her sister had knowledge Givly did not, or she had  had some… visions that she was not privy to. The most disturbing thing to Givly, as she sat, eating some oatmeal that she had bought with her own money, was that Ammareth almost seemed _too_ chipper. She normally wasn't this communicative and proactive this early in the morning. Normally, the best response that Givly would have gotten from her sister was a mumbled acknowledgement of her presence in the room. Yet, this wasn't the case, and that worried the half dragonborn. As she sat there, her overactive mind turning, she realized as her mount came down that his fur was a deeper shade of purple. She momentarily panicked, before realizing— something was giving her a premoniton of what was to come. Celesteia, Anna, and Emmersun all having their hair and fur being brighter, as well as the unusual sky, and Ammerath's mood, seemingly masking a deeper–seated sense of unrest— all these things, they were _warnings_. Givly shook her head. No, no, she tried to reason with herself. But the more she thought about it the more she realized that something was _off_, something that would soon hurt her and her friends… her _family_, both in terms of Ammareth and the other members of the Ten. Givly abandoned her breakfast (which didn't happen very often, given that she was a growing _half dragonborn_) and went to find Anna.

Of course, as one typically would, Givly found the leader of the team in her "office", which also served as a meeting place for her and the Ten. 
"Anna, I need—" She stopped when she saw a kind yet firm hand presented to her.
"Is it an emergency? I'm really tuned in to my work right now, and I cannot suffer any injury to that flow right this—" Anna looked up to see Givly all but hyperventilating out of sheer panic. She didn't look well, either; her normally pale grey, dragon–like skin was pallid, her eyes belying panic that Anna had not realized she had. She put down her pen she had been signing paperwork with and looked her in the eyes, her bright blue eyes, framed by the glasses she almost never wore outside of her office, calming the half-dragonborn somewhat.
"What's wrong, Giv?"
"Something— something's going to happen today, I just know it. First— your hair. It's a bit more shiny, no?"
Anna looked at her hair. Huh, it _was_ shinier. "Is… Is Celesteia's hair… shinier as well?"
"Yes, and not just yours, either… Emmersun's fur too!"
Anna realized— was it? She only really had a basic knowledge of how the magic worked— you gain power, you pull power, you gain massive advantages, some, like hers. But something, intuitively, clicked into place for her.

Both Anna and Givly— understandably so— looked for both Celesteia and Yaereene to confirm that this was not confined to Anna's hair either. It was not— Celesteia's blue hair looked almost as if it were coated with some shiny substance— which was normal–ish for her red hair, but _not_ her blue hair! Something was up, and Celesteia's look of horror was almost palpable. _Something was messing with the magic, and pulling them in_.
