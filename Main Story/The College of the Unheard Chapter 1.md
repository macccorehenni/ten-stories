# Chapter 1 — And thus, the Ten

_A few weeks after the graduation concert and the fight that broke out, Anna has all but been healed. She was in fact death touched by Minden, but by some miracle, the healers were able to save her life. She did not look good at all, but… it was an improvement over the state she was in on the stage. Her skin was blackened, almost as if necrosis had set in or if she were burnt alive. In the meantime, Celesteia, Yae and Amber survived. They were the Misfits, and they stuck together. We rejoin the Misfits as they talked to the Loud Ones._

"Is Anna doing okay, Celeste?" Iris asked. 

"As far as I know, yes." The six students were walking to class on the outskirts of the college. 

"Perhaps we should go to the cafe." Emmersun walked beside the two girls, Amber, Yae, Ilven and the rest of the factions' mounts behind the three.

"No, and slip class? Come on, Emmersun, I thought you better than that. " Celesteia and Iris smiled as Givly came up beside the three, pulling the three into a hug. 

None of the Loud Ones should be anywhere close to death, thought Yae, bitterly. They're only ten and nine, they have so much to live for, yet at least Givly… hell, she's been through too much. 

A young man, mail bag at his side, noticed the six and their mounts, then looked at Ildren, eyes brightened, then approached them. 

"Celesteia? You might want to go talk to the front desk of the hospital." The mail carrier smiled. "I believe your faction leader is ready to talk."

"Awesome. Hey, do you have a moment? I have a letter for the headmistress."

"No, no, no, I got plenty of time. I'll take it, I'm heading there anyways. She's got plenty of… well, let's say, she's got a following, and not the good kind."

"Thanks, man. Hey, are you new here?"

The mail carrier smiled, then said, "Yes. I'm part of the Garrison faction." 

To no one in particular, Givly muttered to herself, "His thoughts seem to say otherwise."

"Watch it, Giv. My power is flaring a bit, I can hear you." Iris was in a dour mood, as were her factionmates. Both the younger Loud Ones had been on edge since the attack, and seemed to have their heads on swivels. 

They had visibly relaxed a bit, though, with the thought that Anna was okay. As Iris bellowed on the stage that night, Anna had become their best friend. 

Ammareth and the rest of the tight-knit group seemed to be handling their emotions well, but the youngest of the Loud Ones were shaken. They felt that Anna's "death" was their fault. They had failed to convince her that she was in danger, that she would be hurt. Iris, especially, had been irritable, often skipping classes "just because". She had bonded with Anna, and the potential loss of her friend? It was unbearable for the normally self-confident ten year old, let alone the nine year old who could _not_ do anything to help. As Amber, Celesteia, Yae and Ammareth thought about it, they came to the same conclusion — that they were unable to help. 

As all six thought about it, the headmistress, Ammadel, approached the factions. 

"Celesteia, Iris? Do you two have a moment? I heard Anna was awake, and have a proposition for you six." the Headmistress said. 

"We've got class to go to, Headmisstress. We can't join you right now. "

Smiling, the Headmistress said, "I already let Professor Ammares know you'll be late. Let's go see Anna together, shall we?"

---

She looked much better. Much of the charring from the failed deathtouch had gone away, except for along her collarbone and her upper arms. 

"Anna? So glad you're okay." Despite her overwhelming sadness over the last few weeks, Celesteia smiled, and broke down crying *happy* tears. "You know, girl, I'm happy for you, and the whole of— of— our *team* is as well."

Suddenly, in a blur of limbs and flight (no doubt assisted by Ammareth's power), Givly jumped into Anna's still tender arms. 

"Ow, Giv! I'm so glad to see you too!" Anna said with a huge yet pained smile on her ashen face. 

They all laughed, and hugged (gently, unlike Givly) their friend. Givly refused to let anyone other than the Misfits and Loud Ones touch her. 

This jubilation was short lived though, as the three people neither Anna nor her friends wanted to see the least walked in through the door— the Mindplayers. 

But, Celesteia noticed, they meant no malice. 

"Tarim, Costa, Caverlly." Anna acknowledged their presence, but Celesteia noticed they were decidedly not welcome. 

"Anna, they're here to apologize. Right, Giv?" She smiled in Anna's direction as Givly nodded at her, her ringlets bouncing. 

"Fine, tell me your—"

Tarim stepped forward, to a growl from Emmersun. 

"Stand down, Emmy." Givly told her mount through bonds, then audibly asked Tarim to begin. 

"Anna, the fight me and my squad took a part of that night— it wasn't okay of us. I, as the new leader of the Mindplayers take full responsibility. As you were probably aware, Minden was not the best person to follow. Power got to their brain _way_, way too easy, and you were a threat to their power. We blindly followed, against our best interests, and that then made us complacent. Amber had the right idea, defecting to you all. We— we understand if you don't want us around." He waited for Anna to respond, and when she didn't respond, Tarim ruefully smiled and turned to leave. 

"Tarim. I understand your conflict. Do you wish to join—" she smiled at Celesteia, then at Givly, "our team?" Tarim, and his faction, all nodded together, unsure.

"Because our friends— the Loud Ones— are allying with us."

"How could you tell? Oh, right. Givly." He smiled. 

"Hell ya! _Finally_ mind reading is useful beyond annoying Iris!" Givly beamed, then turned to Anna as Iris chided her younger factionmate through their bonds. But all present smiled. 

"Well, have you all decided on a team name for us… ten?" Anna said. 

Givly chimed in with, "What about the Ten?"

Iris agreed, and Tarim shrugged his shoulders. "Thus, we are now known as the Ten."

"Now that that's settled, I have my own apology to make on the behalf of the Council." Headmaster Ammadel entered the room. 

"Headmaster, I do accept your apology. But I do want to hear it anyways."

*The Headmistress then explained the Council's reluctance to expel Minden. "Wait and see if they hurt someone," they said. "We don't think they're corrupt, they aren't gonna hurt anyone. We think… they're special." Due to their complacency, Anna got hurt, and she— and the Council— felt like Tarim and the Mindplayers did— complacent for what happened to her.*

"Thusly, you… I resign immediately. I feel you ten are more capable than an adult at managing a college."

"Really?" Iris looked skeptical. 

"Remember we can read minds. Well, mostly me, but anyways." Givly reminded the… former headmaster?

"And what is your power saying, Givly?"

"That you truly want what's best for this school. But… I don't think giving the college over to a bunch of students is gonna help your college, Headmaster."

"Actually…" Tarim smiled. "I know we are more than capable of doing it, Headmaster… or former Headmaster, anyways. Anna is a good leader, she should be the next one. She risked death to protect her friends. She is the new headmistress, and our intrepid team leader."

All the others nodded. 

"My office is yours, then, Anna. Excuse me, *Headmistress* Anna." 

She didn't respond for a moment, then said, "I accept. I feel like Tarim is right, I'm the best fit. I am smart. I am, as Tarim said, willing to lead without the evil powers of Minden. We have never once hurt anyone, unlike the former Mindplayers. I think all ten of us should lead, though. Without Ammereth's time lengthening, Yaereene wouldn't have been able to throw the light, and Iris had to shout to get their attention to allow anything that followed. Everyone here is brave. Mindplayers, your bravery in _apologizing_ is proof enough to be a leader."

---

"Well, what's your first directive as headmistress?" Givly finished petting her mount and reached for Anna's before Ildren and Emmersun both snuggled up. 

"Well, I feel we should replace the  council with all our teammates. As I said earlier, I feel like leading on my own would be unwise. So, thusly, you all are my council now." 

"Awesome, what's next?" Givly said. 

"No one should _ever_ have to go through what we went through— alone, Loud Ones." Anna realized as she said this that she really wasn't alone, now more than ever, but the others? They may need a shoulder to cry on. 

---

Anna and Celesteia grew up in the medium sized town of Eisnore, Celestial Danae. Both had seen one of their parents die. This sent both of them into a spiral of depression, pulling them together at the unlikeliest of moments— as Anna jumped.

This was when they were seventeen. Now, six years later, as adults, they had nowhere to go. But they were travelers after all, and had seen all there was to see in the Celestial Danae— together, as friends. So, they decided to take a tour of Ethryia. The tour took them through the beautiful if small city of Elder's Grove and up to Illford. But looking up at the College as they passed its imposing stature, they realized something was missing in their lives. The aura of the college— fantastic creatures, some different from the Pokémon they had become used to seeing in the Celestial Danaean sky, some similar, all helping their handlers achieve the magic inside them— this inspired the duo to join the college. They got in, mercifully enough, and impressed the jury with their entrance songs.  They had found a rock in a sea of turmoil. They had found a home. They swore to always stick together, even if they are… the Misfits.

--- 

"Ooh, this building has seen better days, Anna." Celesteia stepped over a hole in the floor of the Callendor building. 

"Yeah, and?" Anna smiled. 

"You think we can, you know, live somewhere else and get this place fixed?" Amber chimed in. 

"This is the only building that will fit us all _and_ our group."

"How long until it's fixed?"

"A month. In the meantime we will be sleeping in the offices next to mine. That's still something I'm getting used to saying, by the way. "

The rest of her team sighed. Anna had been super excited about her work, but it took up much of her mornings, what between leading the school and the year in academics. She had taken on a big job with this new title and she knew it. 

"How about we go down to the Iron Blade, for now? Take our mounts out for a ride? All caught up with what I needed to do for work."

"Sure, whatcha think, Emmy?" Givly turned towards her mount and smiled. He bared his fangs gently at his handler, saying "Yes" through the bonds. 

It was a beautiful day, no clouds in sight. Iris agreed, if only to see her friends at the cafe in College City. Amber had felt cooped up, and Yae reckoned some time with her father wouldn't hurt either. The Mindplayers were already out in the Valley practicing maneuvers for the upcoming practice battle, so they would not be coming. Ammereth was the only one who _didn't_ want to come. 

"That's fine by me. Whatcha think, Elly?" Anna smiled, only for Ammereth, who looked up as much towards Anna as Giv, to smile at her and begrudgingly assent to joining. 

"How much longer until that dump of a dorm becomes _ours_ again?" Ammareth complained once everybody was off. 

"A month, no, Anna?" Givly chimed in through her bonds with the rest of the Ten. 

"Yes, Giv. A month. But it should be partially done by next Tuesday, actually." Anna replied. 

"I don't want to live in a dump for two weeks, Anna." It was Costa?

"Thought you three were in the Valley."

"Yes we were, but we decided that we'd see what you all were up to when we saw you, Ildren and the other fliers."

"Ah, we're going to the Iron Blade." Iris sounded happy about this, which confused the Mindplayers, who were not used to her habit of visiting the cafe daily. 

"Chances are Iris knows the barista there," Anna said to the Mindplayers. "She lives there every morning."

"Not _every_ morning, but most." Iris frowned mockingly at her team leader. 

"There vs it is now! Shall we set down, fliers?"

They did as much, and Iris basically hopped off of Elly and skipped into the cafe. Of course, the barista smiled at Iris and greeted her warmly, then turned to the other nine members of the Ten. "And these young gents and ladies are—"

"Iremen? Meet my team."

Iremen nodded at each, adding extra flourish towards Anna when he realized who she was. 

"I've heard much of your team." He turned to Iris and smiled. 

"Your usual, miss?"

"No scone, though, and a water for Elly, please."

Iris jumped excitedly up and down a few times and went to sit in what seemed to be her habitual window, where she could see everyone passing down Resonant Street, the high street of the college's home town. It was all but deserted, which Iris noted for later. Perhaps someone needed their gears kicked into high speed?

"Headmaster Anna! How is it leading this school?"

This is one of the few things that Anna absolutely _hated_ about her new role. "It's going fine, I guess. Only a few weeks really isn't any way to gauge any new job's worth."

"You know, the cafe was affected by the controversy surrounding the battle of Minden, Headmaster."

"No doubt it was for all the Resonant Street businesses. Without any concerts to end the year, it must have pulled people away towards more entertainment in Ilford or even Elder's Grove."

"Oh, my apologies! What are you wanting to drink?"

Anna looked at the menu with everyone else, ordered her drink, then went to go sit on the opposite side of the cafe from Iris. 

---

This cafe reminded Iris of home. While she never got to go to her local cafe back in Celestial Eisnore as a young child, she remembered her foster mother's smell after _she_ went there. 

This cafe— it reminded her of her own immortality and how others would die. Her foster mother died when she was six, leading her to wander on her own until landing at the College. This wandering— feeling alone, feeling like there's nothing she could do to change her situation — allowed her to gravitate towards Anna. She felt this connection, the connection that Anna herself had made with Celesteia years earlier. It was the feeling of _being heard_, of wanting to be heard, it was like a hot chocolate on a warm summer's morning. As Iris sipped on her hot chocolate, hearing her found family enjoying each other, she felt… at peace. Normally, her team would be back at their temporary lodging when she comes here, watching the sun rise over the far turrets of the College— one to soon be her new home for the foreseeable future.

---

Givly felt unsafe in the offices. It seemed like every night that she stayed there, the nightmares got more and more intense. Finally though, Anna got this text from her contractors:

>Final inspection time? Bring all of the Ten, we want to meet them!

This made *everyone* happy, especially Givly. 

"Celeste? Can I have a moment?" Anna asked her friend as they returned to the Callendor building to find it looked— different?

"Hm?" She seemed almost tired, almost weak, like she was doing so much research on the very question that Anna was about to ask. 

"What's Givly's feelings been like? I've noticed she's a bit more… sulky… than normal."

"Yeah, she's stressed about something. We shouldn't probably talk about this in front of her, or even think about it."

Right, Givly was a mind reader. She could read what they were saying no matter if they shielded their bonds or not, as long as she was within a hundred feet, a thousand if she was suspicious and channeled through Emmersun. "We'll talk about this when she goes to Ilford for her dentist's appointment I don't even think she remembers."

"But yes, she has been feeling different over the past month."

"Huh. Here we are, our new home. It looks pretty, doesn't it?"

"This is sooooooooooooooooo cool!" Iris smiled, and went to explore the building. 

Anna smiled, for she knew each of the Ten would find a space of their own. Iris would find a little nest with a bunch of books, most that for an ordinary nine year old would be way too hard to read, and a window overlooking the main college path much like her spot at the cafe. Yae would find much the same, but with shelves to cultivate her feybloom instead of a collection of books. Ildren and the other handlers with flying mounts would find space for them in the large, almost cavern like turrets of the building. Givly had a nest at the top of the tallest turrets.

A builder approched Anna. "Headmaster, how is it?"

Anna smiled. "Good so far. Is there enough rooms for thirty?"

"With all the little spaces you've made for your team themselves, yes, plenty of room to grow."

"Ah, perfect." After making sure Givly wasn't in earshot, telepathically or physically, she whispered, "When's water going in?"

"Today. We're finishing negotiating with Ilford Valley Approch Water Company to bring _this_ much water into the College. Things are going well."

"Good. Hey, I heard you came here too. What power did you manifest?"

"Healing. I know, wasted potential."

"Ah, if your crew gets hurt, then you know—"

"Yep. Imma go check on the water sitch."

"Watch out for the half dragonborn wearing gold. If she learns about this…"

Anna looks behind the contractor— Givly looked back at her, obviously irritated at Anna. 

"Anna, Imma go to the cafeteria. I'm pretty sure we don't have water here yet."

"Sorry about that, Giv. It was out of our control. Hey, I'll see you in a bit, hopefully when the water's on."

"Righty-o."

--- 

Later that afternoon, Anna turned the faucet. "Do we have water, Anna?" Ildren was standing in the main washroom with his handler when she tested the water faucet. The water flowed through, and her sigh of relief was felt through the bonds. Givly hummed through the bond, turning her own faucet in some other part of the house. "Water flowing, yeeeeah. Hm, hm."

"It looooks as much, Ildren. Hopefully we won't need River Water Co out today."

"It does seem like you're much happier, Givly." Ilven noted through bonds. 

"Much happier I am. Loving how you designed my little nook. Makes it easier to snuggle with Emmy." Givly smiled, her enthusiasm for what was now the Ten's new home rubbing off on the others. 

"And I agree with Giv and you— this house, which is really what it is, is magnificent, and hopefully nothing is wrong with it." Amparo chimed in, Dalo chiming in as well with a chirp through the bonds, no doubt in awe at what Anna cooked up. 

Anna was happy for her team. A month or so of waiting for a new home was challenging for her too. But even then, Anna still felt a nagging feeling at her chest— something was wrong with Givly. 

---

Givly was, predictably, snuggling with her tiger when Anna went to find her. "Hey, girl. " Anna started. 

"It's the Parents."

"Excuse me?"

"They were never _my_ parents. My foster parents that I ran away from? I saw them on campus."

"I thought only you and Ammy were sisters. Do you have any other siblings?"

"No, which makes their presence even more upsetting."

"Huh. You think they're here for you and Ammereth?"

"Me, mostly. I think because I'm younger."

"Huh. Well, now we're all safe here, huh."

Givly only smiled at Anna, almost as if Anna was expected to know that that wasn't the case. She then went on, answering Anna's unspoken question, "Yes, I was their 'favorite,' if they really had one. I was always submissive."

She pushed her bangs further across her face, forcing them when they eventually wouldn't budge. 

"So that's why you wear your hair long, huh?"

Celesteia walked in, obviously wielding her power to gauge the emotions before doing so. 

"It must be hard to come to the College to escape one evil pair of people just to have to fight another."

"But the evil pair is _here_, Celeste! And have been off and on all month. Yes, which is why I've not been my usual."

"You're scared," Celesteia stated. "Of course you won't be your usual if you're scared. I'd know that better than anyone else."

Anna smiled at Celesteia, knowing she was referring to how they had met. 

"Yeah. You should rest easy, Giv. We're here for you, even if they do the impossible and attack you, try to hurt you. Hear that, Giv? We are the Ten. We stick together."

Everyone else muttered their assent via bonds, and Iris chimed in with "I won't let them land a single blow on ya!"

Anna felt a lot better. Now she knew why Givly was feeling off, and she vowed over her bedtime routine to always protect her sister from another mother, even if it meant giving her life… again… to an unknown, possibly evil entity who had hurt her. But that, Anna thought as she fell asleep in her building, the one she designed and had built, that concern could wait another day.  Or so she thought, anyways.
