# The Problem

"Mail call!" The mailman who served Callendor entered the huge dorm door and put the mail down in the kitchen. There he found Givly and Deviân smiling, chatting with each other. Deviân had his usual long dreads on, and Givly her normal ponytails, what she called her "tiger buns[^1]". The couple seemed like they were enjoying their tea (hopefully anyways).

"Paulo, hello, morning, how are you?" Givly said.

Paulo nodded at the half dragonborn, wearing an off-shoulder shirt, which Paulo had never expected her to wear.

Ever since he had met her, she had been very much a young lady who was reserved. Over a year ago, she and her team of then ten— hence the name— had just had the worst battle of their life. Paulo had been contracted to lead the building of this dorm— with its expansive space fit for the twenty-one.

He snapped out of the reverie that he had, and complimented Givly on her choice of dress.

"Thanks, Paul." Givly had blushed, and quickly switched the subject. "Any mail for us?"

"Plenty. Two for you, and one for everyone else!"

"Where's the headmaster?"

"Think she's in the conference room." Deviân smiled, and pointed in that direction.
