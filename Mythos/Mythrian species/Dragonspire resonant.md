# The Veildragon-born

>Scientific name: _Draconis resonia_
>Origin: Ethryia (Dragonspires)

## Physical Description

An dragonborn species, resonant are humanoids hailing from the far northern reaches of Popula, the _[[Dragonspires]]_. These areas have birthed all three major Ethryian species, and resonants are especially unique among other species of dragonborn in that they have mostly developed human features. Their face more angular then most humans, they otherwise share the same breadth of features of humans, including temperment. However, they have scales on their upper arms, the color of which depends on their skin tone, but typically are reddish-brown to grey. These scales are hard to the touch, though they vibrate softly due to the powers within. Louder noises around the creatures make the scales glow a darker shade of the color of the scales themselves and vibrate harder. The magic aura that the resonant puts off becomes more visible with the more magic it collects. Overall, compared to an human, their scales create a air of gravitas not present in other species of dragonborn.

## Personality Description

### Wide Range of Temperments; Emotional Training

As mentioned above, due to their genes being mostly that of humans compared to other dragonborn species, their temperment is not fixed into one segment of the spectrum. However, due to their scales' attunement to the [[Veillight|Veil]] and its harmonics, they learn from a long age to control any harsh emotions, leading to most resonant being outwardly peaceful and neutral good.

### When Near the Spires

Their personality changes when near their homeland. They become more agitated as the already thin gap between the Veil and Ethryia thins further, exacerbating the resonance of their scales.

## Magic Description

### Resonant Scales

Resonant scales are imbued with the power of the Veil and its mysterious sonic power. It amplifies sound and channels it, much as a mount can assist its handler in channeling through bonds. Noise charges the scales' powers and stores it in an reservoir for later use. However, this reservoir isn't bottomless— the scales' reservoir has about thirty minutes of loud sounds (louder than 100dBa) before it overloads. When overloaded, the resonant weakens, its magical power weakened especially, and feels dizzy. However, the only way they can lessen this effect is by wielding their power, or moving to a quieter space. If in this state of overload for longer, it risks dying.

### Relationship to the Veil

The Veil powers the resonant's powers, and if near any other break into the Feybraid, such as near Feydor's Forestbreak region, or near the Feytree in the Fey Kingdom, it functions the same as if it is [[Dragonspire resonant#When Near the Spires|near the Spires]]. Otherwise, a base level of resonance is established based on the sound level.
