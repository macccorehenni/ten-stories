# Fragments of the Memories

>Scientific name: _Morphyra paliforma_
>Origin: Mythria ([[Dragonspires]])

## Physical Description

Their initial appearence is that of a grey, othwerwise formless humanoid. However, as it transforms between humanoid forms, it retains a small part of the form it has transferred from. Its major form is the last form it transfered to, though it prides itself on displaying its minor forms, especially if they elicits pleasant memories.

## Personality Description

### Personality Changes with Major Form

It takes on the personality of its major form for the most part, execpt for their favorite pieces of it, as it shapes itself.

### Alignment Stays Neutral Good

However, no matter its personality shift, it remains neutral good.
